# Boundary data and spatial analysis

Generated by `web/tools/boundaries.py` (read-only on `data/` and `docs/`; writes `web/assets/geo/mx.json` and `web/data/derived/geography.json`).
The deck HTML is treated as data: only its `const GEO=` line is parsed.

## (a) What the deck embeds

| Item | Finding |
|---|---|
| Content | 32 state polygons (31 states + CDMX; Baja California and Baja California Sur are separate), 55 rings, **2,731 vertices**; 8 region outlines (7 CENACE control regions + a grey `BAJA`) |
| Source | Not stated in the deck. The names (`Ciudad de México`, `México`) and the 32-feature layout match a generalised Natural Earth / INEGI-derived admin-1 layer; the vertex count shows it was simplified heavily |
| Projection | Pre-projected plate carrée (equirectangular), 40 px per degree of latitude, standard parallel 24 °N (40 · cos 24° = 36.54 px per degree of longitude), origin lon −118.6 / lat 32.9, viewBox 1173 × 740. Recovered by a linear fit of the 2,449 node positions the deck stores against `index.json` lat/lon (max residual 0.06 px) |
| Simplification | One decimal in px (≈ 2.5 km per 0.1 px at this scale); no topology, so shared borders are duplicated per state |
| Regions | Each state is assigned to **one** region by a fixed state→region table (`c` field); the region outline is the union outline of its states. Baja California systems are shown grey and have no values |
| In this page | Same geometry, decoded once to `Path2D` objects (ints = px × 10, delta coded, 32 KB). Values are computed from this project's data |

## (c) Spatial analysis of the 2,659 index nodes

**Coordinates.** 2,599 nodes carry a municipal centroid, 6 inherit the centroid of the node that replaced them, **54 have none** (44 are no longer in the CENACE catalogue, 10 are in it without a municipality centroid; most are Baja California systems or "No Aplica"). Those 54 are never drawn.

**Point in polygon (declared state vs polygon).**

| Geometry | Vertices | Inside declared state | Outside | Of which in a neighbour / in no polygon | Within 5 km of the declared state |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Deck | 2,731 | 2,497 (95.85 %) | 108 | 55 / 53 | 101 (all 108 within 10 km) |
| strotgen (INEGI-derived) | 7,848 | 2,595 (99.62 %) | 10 | 0 / 10 | 9 |
| angelnmara | 9,239 | 2,509 (96.31 %) | 96 | 71 / 25 | 86 |

All 108 deck mismatches lie within 10 km of their declared state: coastal and border municipalities whose centroid falls on the generalised line (Durango 20 of 59, Sinaloa 13, Quintana Roo 11, Tlaxcala 10). **One node, `08PMU-115`, is outside its state in every geometry**: a centroid issue, worth checking at the source. Areas of the deck polygons are within 0.99 of the finer geometry in total (state ratio 0.91–1.08).

**Region vs state.** `index.json` carries the CENACE region of every node. **10 states span several control regions**: Veracruz (Oriental 161, Noreste 6), Sonora (Noroeste 148, Baja California 7), Michoacán (Occidental 93, Central 22), San Luis Potosí (Occidental 89, Noreste 11), Coahuila (Noreste 52, Norte 34), Puebla (Oriental 79, Central 5), Guerrero (Oriental 65, Central 8), Zacatecas (Occidental 42, Norte 5), Hidalgo (Central 27, Occidental 8, Oriental 1, Noreste 1), Morelos (Oriental 26, Central 1). **109 of 2,456 SIN nodes (4.4 %) sit outside the region the map assigns to their state.** Coahuila is the largest case (34 of 86 nodes are Norte while the map paints the whole state Noreste). Region-level colours on this page use the region totals from `analysis.json` (node membership), so they are not affected; state colours mix regions.

## Choropleth by state and region (Aug 2025 – Jul 2026, tracker, today's pesos)

| Measure | Highest | Lowest |
|---|---|---|
| VF, states | Morelos 0.87, Guerrero 0.87, Tamaulipas 0.86 | Sonora 0.53, Chihuahua 0.59, Campeche 0.62 |
| Captured price, states | Quintana Roo 1,195, Yucatán 937, Campeche 901 MXN/MWh | Sonora 127, Chihuahua 185, Sinaloa 288 |
| Spread peak − solar, states | Campeche 1,514, Yucatán 1,486, Quintana Roo 1,445 | Tamaulipas 280, Nuevo León 285, Hidalgo 314 |
| Regions (VF / captured / spread / % covering 714) | Peninsular 0.66 / 1,029 / 1,476 / 98 %; Oriental 0.83 / 657 / 400 / 3 % | Noroeste 0.62 / 179 / 370 / 0 %; Norte 0.64 / 243 / 402 / 0 % |

**Interpretation.** VF and captured price tell different stories. The Peninsula has the *lowest-but-one* VF among regions with data (0.66: strong midday cannibalisation) yet the highest captured price, because the average price is very high (congestion +836 MXN/MWh), and it is the only region where most nodes cover a 714 MXN/MWh cost. Noroeste and Norte combine low VF with a low price level, so no timing fix reaches the cost. Interior regions (Central, Occidental, Noreste) sit at VF 0.83–0.85 and 480–570 MXN/MWh: close to, but under, the printed LCOE. The peak-versus-solar spread is a Peninsula phenomenon (≈ 1,500 MXN/MWh), which is why storage or contracts matter most there. State averages mix regions and node counts (Quintana Roo 47 nodes, Campeche 22), so states with few nodes are noisy.

**Caveat.** Node coordinates are municipal centroids, not substations; a border node can sit in the neighbouring state or the sea, and several nodes share one point. Region and state labels come from CENACE, never from the map.
