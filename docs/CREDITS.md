# Credits (solar resource and PV model)

Solar resource: NASA POWER (MERRA-2 / CERES SYN1deg), long-term climatology; PV model: pvlib-python.

- NASA POWER: https://power.larc.nasa.gov (hourly API v2, community RE). Data are modelled, not measured, and are
  queried at the centre of 1-degree cells.
- pvlib-python (BSD-3): Holmgren, Hansen and Mikofski, JOSS 3(29), 884, 2018.
- Linke turbidity climatology shipped with pvlib (SoDa/Remund et al.).
